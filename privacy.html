What we collect

  • Your email address, if you ask for the playbook. That is the only thing that form collects.
  • Your name, email, home airport, destination, travel timing, approximate points balance and any notes you add, if you complete the questionnaire. You choose what to put in the notes field — please do not put account numbers, card numbers or passwords there.
  • Booking details if you schedule a call: your name, email and the time you chose.
  • Basic usage data if analytics are enabled: pages viewed, approximate region, and referring site. Not your identity.

We do not ask for, and you should never send us, your card numbers, banking logins, frequent-flyer passwords or Social Security number. If a conversation ever needs access to an account, we will tell you how to share it safely rather than by email.

What we do with it

  • Send you the playbook you asked for.
  • Prepare for and run the call you booked.
  • Reply to you, and follow up about work you have engaged us for.
  • Understand which parts of the site are useful.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.
